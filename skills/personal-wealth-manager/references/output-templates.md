# Output templates

## Compact decision output

Default to this unless the user requests a complete review:

1. **Current decision** — what is being decided and which money pool is affected.
2. **Known and unknown** — only facts and uncertainties that can change the decision.
3. **Three key numbers** — choose the most relevant, such as actual surplus, safe runway, authorized risk exposure, stress loss, or sleep-income coverage.
4. **Core diagnosis** — the key constraint and its evidence.
5. **One path** — what to reduce, preserve, validate, or build; do not manufacture action without evidence.
6. **Next step** — one reversible action, evidence, and a price-independent review trigger; add a review date only when it is supported by the decision context.

Tag consequential statements as **Fact**, **Calculation**, **Judgment**, or **User decision** when the distinction is not obvious.

4. the next one to three questions;
5. one safe action that does not depend on missing data.
