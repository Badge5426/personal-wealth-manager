# Mainland China, privacy, and decision safety

## Jurisdiction

Default to an individual resident in mainland China. Do not generalize a rule from another jurisdiction.

When a recommendation depends on current rules, verify primary or official sources at the time of use. Relevant authorities may include:

- National Financial Regulatory Administration and its local offices;
- People's Bank of China and State Administration of Foreign Exchange;
- China Securities Regulatory Commission, exchanges, and fund/product disclosures;
- State Taxation Administration;
- Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and local social-security authorities;
- National Healthcare Security Administration and local authorities;
- official national laws and judicial interpretations;
- the specific bank, broker, insurer, fund manager, or product contract.

Use official product documents for fees, risk level, redemption, lockup, guarantee status, and suitability. Marketing labels are not evidence of capital protection.

## Time-sensitive topics requiring verification

- deposit rates and whether a product is a deposit;
- deposit-insurance scope and current limit;
- tax treatment of securities, funds, rent, royalties, platform income, gifts, or cross-border assets;
- foreign-exchange conversion and remittance rules;
- social insurance, medical insurance, pension, and contribution rules;
- insurance exclusions, renewal, cash value, and claim conditions;
- marital property, gifts, title registration, and ownership;
- settlement, redemption, suspension, and delisting rules.

State the “as of” date and distinguish official fact from inference.

## Privacy

Never request:

- passwords, verification codes, PINs, or private keys;
- complete bank or brokerage account numbers;
- full identity numbers, addresses, or policy numbers;
- remote-control access or permission to move money.

Ask the user to redact screenshots. Summaries should use descriptive account labels, not identifiers.

Default to processing only within the current conversation. Do not persist a personal ledger unless the user asks for a file and understands what will be stored.

Never publish or commit a user's real balances, transactions, holdings, statements, tax records, pay records, screenshots, or identifiers to a public repository. When open-sourcing the skill itself, publish only generic instructions, scripts, tests, and synthetic examples.

## Consent and ownership

Identify:

- individual versus joint decision;
- legal ownership versus practical control;
- earmarked funds versus genuinely investable funds;
- whose loss capacity is affected.

Do not advise concealment, account fragmentation to evade a co-owner's decision, or unilateral use of jointly relevant money. Help the user present transparent scenarios and negotiate a mutually acceptable risk envelope.

## Professional boundary

This skill supplies education, organization, arithmetic, and decision structure. It does not replace a licensed investment adviser, lawyer, tax adviser, insurance professional, or medical professional.

Escalate when:

- ownership or marital-property treatment is disputed;
- a tax or regulatory consequence is material and unclear;
- the product is complex, leveraged, private, structured, or opaque;
- the user may lose essential housing, medical care, or livelihood;
- there are signs of fraud, coercion, unauthorized trading, or elder financial abuse.

## Language discipline

Avoid:

- “guaranteed”, “risk-free”, “sure to recover”, or “will beat inflation”;
- diagnosing a product from recent return alone;
- equating dividend yield with total return;
- presenting a model allocation as the only correct answer;
- pretending estimates are exact.

Use:

- “under these assumptions”;
- “provisional classification”;
- “requires current official verification”;
- “the arithmetic supports, but does not recommend”;
- “the user decides after understanding tradeoffs”.
